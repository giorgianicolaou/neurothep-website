The Problem

Traditional Approaches
Have Failed

Reductionist strategies ignore the biological complexity of Central Nervous System diseases. Single-target modulation is insufficient.

"ApoE4 isoform increases synapse-associated oligomeric amyloid-β (Aβ) compared with apoE3... enhancing synaptic toxicity." — Koffie et al., Brain 2012

Small molecules and monoclonal antibodies directed at Tau and Aβ plaques have repeatedly failed — and plaques exist even in cognitively normal brains.

Scientific Foundation

Science of ApoE4:
Synapse-Centric Pathology

Lead molecules demonstrate 5 distinct therapeutic activities in a single compound — targeting the root cause, not just symptoms.

1

Activates Synapses

Specifically activates synapses in ApoE4 brain. Note: No effect on ApoE3 synapses — precision targeting confirmed.

2

Modulates Interaction

Disrupts the harmful binding between ApoE4 and Aβ, preventing synaptic targeting and downstream toxicity.

3

Inhibits Fibrils

Blocks Aβ1-42 fibril formation, reducing the overall amyloid burden driving neurodegeneration.

4

Modulates Genes

Regulates genes associated with inflammatory processes, dampening neuroinflammation central to AD progression.

5

Enhances Energy

Boosts mitochondrial activity, addressing metabolic deficits in AD neurons that accelerate cognitive decline.
